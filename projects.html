Contextual Sarcasm Detection

Undergraduate Thesis · 2025

Developed an encoder–encoder cross-attention model for contextual sarcasm detection in social media conversations, jointly modeling comments and their parent context. Introduced sarcasm-adaptive pretraining strategies to capture subtle linguistic incongruities, with strong results on political sarcasm benchmarks. Extended into a journal manuscript currently under review.

Technologies: Python, PyTorch, Transformers, ELECTRA, Cross-Attention

NLP Sarcasm Thesis

Clickbait Content Detection

2024 – Present

Built a multimodal (text + image) system for YouTube clickbait detection by combining language embeddings with CLIP visual features and incongruence-aware fusion. Extended into the YTClickbait21K dataset and ICFNet framework, with multimodal LLM comparison and explainability analysis. Related manuscripts are under review at Scientific Data and Information Processing & Management.

Technologies: Python, PyTorch, CLIP, RoBERTa, ConvNeXtV2, Multimodal RAG / VLMs

Multimodal Clickbait Dataset

Medical Triage Classification

2024 – 2025

Developed a BERT-based hybrid model that combines free-text triage notes with numeric clinical features to classify cases as critical, urgent, or non-urgent. Integrated regex and medical dictionaries for rare terminology handling, achieving 83% accuracy as a foundation for AI-assisted clinical decision support.

Technologies: Python, Custom BERT, Regex, Medical Dictionaries, Scikit-learn

US Election 2024 Data Analysis

2025

Analyzed election-related Twitter data with semantic search, topic modeling, and GraphRAG. Clustered embeddings using t-SNE and HDBSCAN to surface communities, influence patterns, and misinformation signals—exploring NLP and graph-based AI as an alternative lens to traditional polling.

Technologies: Python, LLMs, GraphRAG, t-SNE, HDBSCAN, Semantic Search
