Research Interests & Projects

Research Interests

  • Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Computational Linguistics, focusing on language understanding, text classification, and context-aware models.
  • Medical Text Analysis and Healthcare Informatics, leveraging AI to improve clinical decision-making and patient data analytics.
  • Multimodal Learning and Contextual Embeddings, integrating diverse data types like images, text, and signals for richer AI applications.
  • Explainable AI and Model Interpretability, ensuring transparency and trustworthiness in AI-driven systems and predictive models.
  • Topic Modeling, Semantic Search, and Representation Learning for uncovering hidden patterns and enhancing information retrieval.
  • Bengali Linguistics and Low-Resource Language Processing, addressing challenges in underrepresented languages through computational approaches.

Projects

Contextual Sarcasm Detection (Undergraduate Thesis)

Developed an encoder-encoder cross-attention model for detecting contextual sarcasm. Combined CLIP for image encoding and mBERT for text processing, achieving 95% accuracy. Currently refining a custom multimodal architecture for enhanced performance.

Clickbait Content Detection

Built a tool to detect clickbait on social media using multimodal analysis. Integrated CLIP and mBERT models, achieving 95% accuracy in initial evaluations. Currently extending the dataset and refining model reliability.

Medical Triage Classification

Developed a BERT-based model for classifying medical triage notes. Implemented regex and medical dictionaries for preprocessing and achieved 83% accuracy. Also explored multimodal and EDBERT-based enhancements for improved classification.

US Election Data Analysis

Analyzed Twitter posts using topic modeling, BERT-based semantic search, and GraphRAG. Clustered embeddings with t-SNE and HDBSCAN for extracting election-related insights. Proposed an alternative to traditional exit polls, with ongoing evaluation.
