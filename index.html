About Me

Passionate researcher in Computer Science and Engineering

I have recently completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology. I have achieved a CGPA of 3.42 out of 4.00. I have several publications 5 conference papers and 1 journal paper. I have also worked at Makebell as an AI Engineer for 4 months (june 2025 - september 2025) where I was responsible for designing a domain specific Translation model for legal documents translation following users writing style, and term specific vocabulary. My research interests encompass a broad spectrum, including Natural Language Processing, Digital Humanities, Computational Linguistics, Agentic AI, Medical text analysis and LLM's behavior analysis.

Prior to my educational experience, I worked as a Research Assistant under the supervision of Md. Harun Or Rashid and Md Azmain Yakin Srizon.
