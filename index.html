About Me

Researcher in AI, NLP, and multimodal systems

I recently completed my BSc in Computer Science and Engineering at Rajshahi University of Engineering and Technology (CGPA 3.42/4.00) and currently work as a remote Research Assistant at Qatar University. My research centers on large language models (LLMs), multimodal analysis, agentic AI, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), privacy-preserving machine learning, and medical text analysis.

I have published 1 journal article and 6 conference papers, with 4 manuscripts currently under review at venues including Information Processing & Management, Scientific Data (Nature Portfolio), Journal of Big Data, and IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security. Previously, I worked as an AI Engineer at Makebell Ltd (legal-domain RAG translation) and as a Junior Research Scientist at GPHRF (multimodal health biomarkers).

Earlier research was conducted under the supervision of Md. Harun Or Rashid and Md Azmain Yakin Srizon at Young Learners Research Lab and the Computational Linguistics Research Lab.
